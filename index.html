Barnhold Acrelords — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026 · Version 1.0

Barnhold Acrelords is a small independently made game. We collect as little as possible: the game has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. This page explains exactly what is stored and what is sent over the network.

Who we are

Barnhold Acrelords ("the game") is developed and published by Dave Armstrong ("we", "us", "our"). For the purposes of the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation we are the data controller for the limited data described below.

The short version

  • Your progress is saved on your own device. We do not have a copy of it.
  • Playing offline sends nothing anywhere.
  • Playing online creates a random anonymous player ID through Unity Gaming Services. It is not linked to your name, email address or any account.
  • We do not show ads, and we do not use advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location or photos.

What the game stores on your device

The game saves your progress locally using your platform's standard application storage. This includes:

  • which levels you have cleared and which are unlocked;
  • your renown balance and upgrade levels;
  • your saved loadout and the boosts you currently hold;
  • chest timers and daily-chest counters;
  • your audio, display and control settings.

This data never leaves your device unless you copy it yourself. Uninstalling the game deletes it. Any in-game "reset progress" option deletes it too.

What happens when you play online

Online multiplayer uses Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Lobby and Relay), operated by Unity Technologies. When you play online:

  • Anonymous player ID. The game signs you in anonymously. This creates a random identifier for your installation. It is not connected to an email address, social account or real-world identity, and you are never asked to create an account.
  • Lobby details. So that two players can find each other, the lobby holds a short join code and the match settings (map seed, game version and which side each player defends). This is shared only with the other player in that match.
  • Connection data. During a match, game traffic travels through Unity's relay servers. Players connect through the relay rather than directly to each other, so the other player does not learn your IP address. Unity does process connection metadata (such as IP address, region and timings) as the infrastructure provider, under Unity's privacy policy.

We do not run our own servers. We do not store match history, chat logs, player profiles or any other record of your online matches. Once a match ends, its lobby is closed and nothing is kept by us.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or postal address.
  • No account, password or social-media login.
  • No precise or approximate location.
  • No contacts, calendar, photos, files, camera, microphone or clipboard access.
  • No advertising identifier, and no advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No third-party analytics or crash-reporting service.
  • No in-game chat, voice chat or user-generated content, so we never receive anything you type or say.

The game does not request any Android permission beyond network access, and it never reads data belonging to other apps on your device.

Payments

The game does not process payments and does not contain in-app purchases. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated before the change ships, and any payment would be handled entirely by the store you bought the game from — we would never see your card details.

Third parties

  • Unity Technologies — provides the online services described above and, as the engine vendor, may receive standard technical information when the game connects to those services. Unity privacy policy.
  • The store you downloaded the game from (for example Google Play) handles your store account, downloads and any reviews. That is between you and the store and is governed by its own privacy policy.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, and we do not share it for advertising or cross-context behavioural targeting.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us and we will delete it.

How long we keep things

  • On your device: your save data stays until you uninstall the game or reset your progress.
  • Anonymous player ID: held by Unity Gaming Services for as long as the service associates it with your installation. We hold no copy of it.
  • Emails you send us: kept only as long as needed to answer you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to receive a copy of it. You also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

In practice there is very little for us to act on, because we hold no personal data about you. To erase everything the game has stored, uninstall it or use the in-game reset. If you would like an anonymous online identifier removed, or want to exercise any of the rights above, email us at the address below and we will help.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not discriminate against anyone for exercising their privacy rights.

Security

Online traffic is carried over encrypted connections (TLS/DTLS) between the game and Unity's services. No system is perfectly secure, but because we hold no personal data of our own, there is nothing for an attacker to obtain from us.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts collecting something new — for example optional accounts, cloud saves, or a crash-reporting service — we will update this page and change the date at the top before that version ships. Material changes will also be mentioned in the game or in the release notes for the update.
